## What this is about

Each piece of work (an issue, a why agent, a solution agent, a helper) runs as background sessions in a worktree of its own: a folder with its own checkout of the repo. When the work ends, the script must stop its sessions, remove the worktree and its branch, and move its notes to `state/done/`.

Four commands each write those steps themselves, in their own order (`merge`, `why-done`, `solve-done`, and the helper cleanup `status` runs), and the script works out where a worktree is from a list of two older folders typed into it. On 9 October `merge 82` removed issue 82's worktree first, then looked for its sessions in the wrong folder, and left its reviewer running:

```
$ orchestrate.sh merge 82
merged logoslang#82 into dev at 27ed63c; branch and worktree gone; issue closed
$ orchestrate.sh status
  logoslang-issue-82-review-7	5cf32136	idle	done
```

The same causes explain three more: a helper resumed after a restart can have its worktree removed while it still runs; a worktree is lost if the worktree folder setting changes; and a finished helper's session is left in the list (question 1).
