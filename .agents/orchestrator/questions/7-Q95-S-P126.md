## 1. How should I hear your saved answers?
**Decision:** which of three fixes to build · **Recommended:** (a) · **Why:** nothing has to be restarted or remembered

---

Today I hear a save only through the watch, which runs inside one of my own tool calls and stops after 30 minutes. If I forget to restart it, your saves wait until you ask. That happened with Q79, Q89 and Q90 (problem P126).

Claude Code has two features that fix this. A skill can register its own **hooks** (small scripts Claude Code runs by itself, for example each time my turn ends), so you edit no settings file. And one kind of hook can **wake me even when I am idle**.

- (a) **The skill wakes and guards me by itself.** Each time my turn ends, a hook starts the watch; your save wakes me at once, idle or not. A second hook keeps my turn from ending while a file holds a save I have not taken in. A third blocks the question dialog that held my turn for 8 minutes on Q79. The 30-minute restarts go away. About two hours of work; Claude Code may ask you once to approve the hooks.
- (b) **Keep today's watch, but enforce the restarts.** Same guard, but I restart the watch every 30 minutes, which wakes me up to 48 times a day for nothing.
- (c) **Only switch on the guard I built.** You add one line to your settings file. It catches a watch that is down when my turn ends, but not the Q79 case, and the 30-minute restarts stay.

Recommended: (a), because it removes the cause instead of checking for it.
